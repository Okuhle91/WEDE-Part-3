Our Story

The FrostFlow Industries was found in 2024 October with a simple mission of providing local taverns around KZN.

What started as a small local operation has expanded into the one of the trusted suppliers across the country.

When this business started it employed eight individuals from Bhulwa, after one year the OFFI has employed 40% of individuals living around Bhulwa.

As OFFI is now a big commercial business working with big retail markets such as Shoprite Liquior and Total Energies,

it is now looking forward on a goal of providing it's products to every retail Liquior stores in South Africa and Petrol Stations as well.

Quality and Purity

The source of water used to create the ice tubes purified(undergone borehole testing).

Before each and every production cycle of ice the equipment used that's going to be in-contact with water is cleaned/sanitized.

The freezing cylinders also cleaned with food-grade sanitizer and rinsed with purified water.
